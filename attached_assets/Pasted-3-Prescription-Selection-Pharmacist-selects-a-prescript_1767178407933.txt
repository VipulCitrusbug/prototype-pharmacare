3. Prescription Selection
Pharmacist selects a prescription from the prioritized queue.


Prescription status moves to “In Review”.


System locks the prescription to prevent parallel processing conflicts.



4. Prescription Intake & Data Capture (AI Prescription Intelligence)
If the prescription is scanned, faxed, or handwritten:


AI OCR + NLP extracts prescription data.


Structured fields are populated (drug name, dosage, frequency, duration).


Low-confidence fields are clearly highlighted for pharmacist review.


If the prescription is already structured (eRx or manual entry):


Data is prefilled and ready for validation.


Decision Point:
The pharmacist reviews all extracted fields.


Pharmacist confirms, edits, or corrects any AI-extracted data.


Corrections are saved and used to improve future AI accuracy.



5. Prescription Validation
Pharmacist validates:


Drug correctness


Dosage and frequency alignment


Completeness of instructions


Validation is a mandatory human action.


System prevents progression if required fields are incomplete or unresolved.



6. Dispensing Preparation
Pharmacist proceeds to dispensing preparation.


Inventory availability is confirmed.


Expiry-sensitive stock is surfaced to guide selection.


Prescription status updates to “Preparing”.



7. Dispensing Execution
Pharmacist completes dispensing actions.


Any deviations, substitutions, or overrides trigger documentation requirements.


Prescription status updates to “Dispensed”.



8. Documentation (AI Documentation Assistant)
AI Documentation Assistant auto-drafts:


Dispensing notes


Override justifications (if applicable)


Draft content is presented in editable form.


Pharmacist reviews, edits if needed, and explicitly confirms documentation.


Decision Point:
Pharmacist approves documentation.


System timestamps and locks the entry for audit purposes.



9. Prescription Completion
Prescription status updates to “Ready for Pickup” or “Out for Delivery”, based on order type.


Patient notification is triggered by the system (outside pharmacist action).


Prescription is removed from active pharmacist queue.



10. Continuous Queue Re-evaluation (AI-Assisted)
AI Workload Prioritization re-evaluates the queue in real time.


Newly added or aging prescriptions are re-ranked.


Alerts are raised if backlog thresholds are crossed
